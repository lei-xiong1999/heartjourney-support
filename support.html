Help & Support / 帮助与支持

Heart Journey helps you review heart-rate changes during everyday activities using available historical readings from Apple Health. It does not measure heart rate, diagnose conditions, or infer emotions.

心旅结合 Apple 健康中可用的历史读数，帮助你回顾日常活动期间的心率变化。不自行测量、不诊断、不识别情绪。

Missing readings / 没有读数

Check the selected time range in Apple Health, read permissions, and Watch-to-iPhone synchronization. Sampling frequency varies. No particular density or uninterrupted timeline is guaranteed.

请检查健康中的对应时段、读取权限及手表同步状态。采样频率可能变化，不保证固定密度或完整连续曲线。

Lifetime Membership / 永久会员

The free version saves up to 3 journeys at a time, including demos. Deleting a journey frees a slot. Reservations do not count. Lifetime Membership is a one-time non-consumable purchase with no recurring charge. The App Store displays your local price. Use Settings → Lifetime Membership & Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Existing journeys remain accessible after a refund; the free limit applies to new saves.

免费版同时保存最多 3 段心旅（包括演示），删除后可腾出名额，预订不计入。永久会员为一次性非消耗型购买，不自动续费，价格以 App Store 当地显示为准。使用原购买 Apple 账户，在设置中的永久会员入口恢复购买。退款后已有心旅仍可访问，新增保存恢复免费限制。

Privacy & deletion / 隐私与删除

Settings can clear local journeys and reservations without changing Apple Health originals. Revoking permission does not delete imported copies. Uninstalling removes local app data. Exported copies need separate deletion.

设置可清空本地心旅与预订，不影响健康原始数据。撤销权限不会删除已导入副本。卸载会删除本地应用数据，已导出文件需要另行删除。

Reservations / 预订

A calendar reservation saves an event time for later import. It does not start Watch measurement, send reminders, or import automatically.

日历预订保存事件时间供事后导入，不启动手表测量、不发送提醒、不自动导入。
